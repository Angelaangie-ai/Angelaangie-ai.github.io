Continuum Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Continuum is a health and longevity application designed to help people bring together information from health records, wearable devices, uploaded reports, and other sources they choose to connect. This Privacy Policy explains how Continuum handles information when you use the application or connect supported services.

Information you choose to connect

Depending on the features you use, Continuum may process information such as:

  • Health records, including laboratory results, diagnoses, medications, procedures, immunizations, encounters, and related clinical information.
  • Wearable and fitness information, including sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, activity, workouts, recovery, readiness, and related metrics.
  • Information from Apple Health, WHOOP, Oura, Epic or other health-data services that you explicitly authorize.
  • Health files or reports that you choose to upload.
  • Basic account and device information necessary to operate the application.

How Continuum uses information

Continuum uses connected information to provide the features you request, including organizing your health history, displaying health trends, building your personal baseline, and generating personalized insights or predictions. Continuum may also use de-identified or aggregated information to improve the product, evaluate performance, and develop new features where permitted by applicable law and user consent.

Connected services

When you connect a third-party service such as Epic, WHOOP, Oura, or Apple Health, Continuum accesses only the information permitted by the authorization you grant. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms governing their systems.

Storage and security

Continuum is designed to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of health information. Where supported, authentication credentials are stored separately from health records, and locally stored health records may use device-level protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so Continuum cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

Continuum does not sell personal health information. Continuum does not share personal health information with advertisers. Information may be disclosed to service providers that help operate the product, when necessary to provide a feature you request, when you direct or consent to the disclosure, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can choose which supported services to connect. You can revoke third-party access through the relevant provider and, where available, disconnect a source from Continuum. Disconnecting a service stops future access to that service, although previously stored information may remain until it is deleted according to the product's data-retention behavior.

Data retention and deletion

Continuum retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the product, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security. Where the application provides a disconnect or deletion control, Continuum will apply that control to the relevant locally stored or connected data as described in the product.

Children

Continuum is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Continuum may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Continuum team through the developer contact information associated with the application or its connected service listing.
